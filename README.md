📜 The Digital Manifesto

"The unexamined code is not worth executing."

I am a Digital Alchemist navigating the intersection of Machine Learning and Human Existentialism. Rather than merely building software, I seek to understand the Epistemology of Algorithms—how data becomes knowledge and how knowledge shapes our reality.

🌌 Seeking: The "Ghost in the Machine" through Deep Learning and AI.

🏛️ Philosophy: Stoicism meets Silicon. I believe in clean code as a form of moral integrity.

🔭 Current Inquiry: Engineering ethical AI systems and architectural beauty in Web Applications.

⚡ Aphorism: Technology is the campfire around which we tell our modern stories.
